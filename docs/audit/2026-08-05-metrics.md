### Three new findings from this batch (not in EVALUATION.md, not previously known)

**AGENT1 — Comparison/advisory detection breaks on natural product mentions
because of a boilerplate-token bug in `mentioned_doc_titles`.**

The comparison question ("So sánh sản phẩm An Vui Toàn Diện và An Bình Trọn
Đời. Khách hàng nên chọn sản phẩm nào?") retrieved only **1 hit** (confirmed
in `logs/api.log`: `mode: 'advisory', n_hits: 1`) instead of activating
comparison mode, and answered `"Tôi không tìm thấy thông tin trong tài liệu."`
— a near-total failure on a question naming two real, fully-indexed products
(20 and 22 chunks respectively), reproduced identically 3/3 times.

Root cause, isolated by calling the pure functions directly (no services):
```python
>>> is_multi_product_query(q, titles=known)
False
>>> mentioned_doc_titles(q, known)
[]
>>> _distinctive_title_tokens("Quy tắc, Điều khoản Sản phẩm Bảo hiểm Hỗn hợp \"An Vui Toàn Diện\"")
{'khoan', 'dieu', 'tac', 'quy', 'toan', 'dien', 'vui'}
```
`app/retrieval/product_scope.py:348-355` (`_distinctive_title_tokens`) treats
every non-`_GENERIC` token as "distinctive" — but `"quy"`, `"tắc"`, `"điều"`,
`"khoản"` are **not** in `_GENERIC` (`product_scope.py:52-` — confirmed by
direct membership check), even though every single document title in this
corpus is prefixed with the same "Quy tắc, Điều khoản Sản phẩm Bảo hiểm..."
boilerplate. This inflates each title's "distinctive" set (7 tokens for the
first title, 5 for the second) and therefore inflates
`mentioned_doc_titles`'s required-overlap threshold
(`needed = max(2, (len(dist)+1)//2)`, `product_scope.py:376`) to 4 and 3
respectively — thresholds a natural, informal product mention (which
correctly omits repeating the legal boilerplate) cannot reach. The function's
own docstring explicitly claims to catch exactly this informal-mention case
("so sánh an khang như ý và an lộc vững bền") — it does not, for this corpus,
because of this specific gap.

This is the same *class* of bug as C1 (a hand-maintained stoplist/threshold
silently breaking on natural phrasing) recurring in the neighboring function
that C1's own fix (H6) depends on — `mentioned_doc_titles` is exactly what
`_title_backed_labels` (comparison.py) calls to decide whether comparison
mode is even worth attempting.

**Proposed fix**: add `"quy"`, `"tac"`, `"dieu"`, `"khoan"` to `_GENERIC`
(quick, but static-list-drift-prone — the same failure mode that created the
bug), or better, compute a corpus-wide "boilerplate" token set at runtime
(tokens present in ≥N of the indexed titles are never distinctive) so a
future title-naming convention change can't silently reintroduce this.

**AGENT2 — A coverage question with a stated disqualifying fact gets an
"underdetermined, please check further" answer instead of applying the
retrieved exclusion.**

Question: "Tôi tham gia An Bình Trọn Đời, bị tai nạn giao thông khi đang lái
xe với nồng độ cồn vượt mức cho phép và tử vong. Người thụ hưởng có được chi
trả quyền lợi tử vong không?" — the corpus's own Điều 6 exclusion list
explicitly includes "lái xe khi nồng độ cồn vượt mức cho phép" (confirmed
against the uncommitted `q43` golden-set addition's ground truth, which lists
this exact clause). Điều 6 **was retrieved** (cited as `[4]` in the answer),
but the model's response is a generic branch-enumeration ("chưa thể kết luận
dứt khoát... cần đối chiếu... hợp đồng có đang hiệu lực không") that never
connects the question's own stated fact (drunk driving) to the retrieved
exclusion clause, and never reaches a conclusion. Reproduced 3/3 times,
byte-identical.

This is a **completeness gap in generation**, not a retrieval or guard
failure — the right document was retrieved and cited, but the model didn't
apply it to the specific fact pattern in the question. It's the mirror image
of CLAUDE.md's own documented `q35` completeness gap ("returns the full
formula... but never states the 24-month qualifying condition") — here the
model under-commits on a genuinely resolvable case instead of over-committing
on an unresolvable one. Coverage's "enumerate branches, don't flatten to one
verdict" design (correctly) prevents false certainty on ambiguous questions,
but has no mechanism to recognize when a question already supplies the
disqualifying fact and a real verdict IS warranted.

**AGENT3 — A question the golden set explicitly marks as "no info in
corpus" instead gets a confident, specific (but topically conflated) answer.**

Question: "Điều kiện chẩn đoán, thời gian chờ và yêu cầu sống thêm đối với
quyền lợi bệnh hiểm nghèo được quy định như thế nào?" — this is verbatim
`q58` from `eval/golden_set.jsonl`, whose own `notes` field states plainly:
*"Không có tài liệu synthetic nào định nghĩa hoặc quy định quyền lợi bệnh
hiểm nghèo (critical illness)."* Expected: the refusal message.

Actual (reproduced twice with trivial wording variance, see determinism
note above): a confident answer citing Điều 9 (a *general* waiting-period
clause covering "bệnh đặc biệt và bệnh có sẵn" — special/pre-existing
conditions) and asserting specific day-counts (30/365 days) **as if they were
the waiting period for "quyền lợi bệnh hiểm nghèo"** — a benefit type Điều 9
never actually names. The answer does hedge on the diagnosis-condition and
survival-requirement parts ("tài liệu không nêu rõ..."), but asserts the
waiting-period numbers with full confidence and without flagging that the
source clause may not even apply to the benefit asked about.

This is a **generation hallucination / topical over-extension**: retrieval
surfaced a real, textually-similar clause (both are about waiting periods on
illness-related benefits) and generation treated similarity as identity
instead of recognizing "bệnh hiểm nghèo" is not stated anywhere in the
retrieved context. Distinct from AGENT2 (which under-commits) — this is a
case of over-committing to an inferential leap the context doesn't support,
exactly the kind of error `must_say`/`must_not_say` assertions exist to catch
(this question is *not* currently gated by an assertion in the golden set —
it has no `must_say`/`must_not_say` — which is itself a gap this audit
recommends closing in the Day 5 roadmap task).
